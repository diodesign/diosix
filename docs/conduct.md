# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

As members, contributors, and maintainers of the [Diosix](https://diosix.org)
project, we pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for
everyone—regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* Showing empathy and kindness toward other people.
* Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
* Taking responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
* Public or private harassment.
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
* Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The project lead clarifies and enforces our standards of acceptable behavior
and takes fair corrective action in response to any behavior deemed
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

The project lead has the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that do
not align with this code of conduct, and communicates reasons for moderation
decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within all project spaces, and also applies when
an individual officially represents the Diosix project in public spaces—such as
using an official project email address, posting via an official social media
account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline
event.

## Enforcement

Report instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior to
the Diosix [project lead](mailto:chrisw@diosix.org). The project lead reviews
and investigates all complaints promptly and fairly, and respects the privacy
and security of the person reporting any incident.

## Enforcement guidelines

The project lead follows these community impact guidelines when determining
consequences for violations of this code of conduct:

### Correction

This level covers inappropriate language or other behavior considered
unprofessional or unwelcome in the community. It results in a private, written
warning from the project lead explaining the nature of the violation and why
the behavior was inappropriate, and a public apology may be requested.

### Warning

This level covers a violation through a single incident or series of actions.
It results in a formal warning with consequences for continued behavior and no
unsolicited interaction with the people involved—including those enforcing the
code of conduct—for a specified period; violating these terms may lead to a
temporary or permanent ban.

### Temporary ban

This level covers a serious violation of community standards, including
sustained inappropriate behavior. It results in a temporary ban from any
interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period,
and violating those terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### Permanent ban

This level covers a pattern of violating community standards, including
sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or disparagement
of classes of individuals. It results in a permanent ban from any public
interaction within the community.
